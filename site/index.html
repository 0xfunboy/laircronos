About

What LAIR is

LAIR is an autonomous AI Agent built to operate in the Cronos ecosystem. She blends market, social and on-chain data to surface momentum tokens, produce instant technical snapshots and deliver actionable context in seconds. The stack is battle-tested and designed for scale and reliability.

Core Product

Agent-driven trading workflow (what the dApp makes visible)

LAIRTrading / LAIRTrack execution loop

The core product is not only content generation: it is the agent pipeline that parses trade setups, tracks market prices, manages lifecycle transitions, and applies risk controls while exposing everything in a public dashboard.

The LAIR dApp is the operational surface for this engine: you can inspect pending entries, opened trades, closed results, chart overlays and execution timestamps in a single place.

Execution Lifecycle

1
Signal Parsing
Agent output / social posts are parsed into entry, TP, SL and side.
2
Pending Validation
Trades stay pending until entry conditions are met or are discarded if stale.
3
Open Position Tracking
Live prices update unrealized PnL and chart overlays in the dApp.
4
Managed Risk Exit
TP / stop / managed trailing logic closes the trade and preserves execution history.

Guide

How to interact with LAIR on X

Tag @LAIRcronos followed by an action and a $TICKER or contract address (CA).
CHART, ANALYSIS and FUNDAMENTAL require a ticker or CA. You can also request NEWS or SENTIMENT without symbols.

Commands

CHART $CRO 1h • hourly chart with indicators
ANALYSIS 0x… • blended on-chain + trading + sentiment
FUNDAMENTAL $ETH • market cap, liquidity, supply
PRICE $TICKER • real-time quote with quick read
NEWS • market-moving updates
SENTIMENT • live investor mood

Tips

Use clear tickers or paste the CA for new listings. Add timeframe for charts (e.g., 1h, 4h, 1d). Combine with your DYOR. LAIR outputs analytics, not financial advice.
